By submitting a contribution, you represent that, to the best of your
knowledge:

1. you have the legal right to submit the contribution;

3. the contribution does not knowingly include material that is subject to

the intended use in Ashen Chronicle have been verified.

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, contributed Source Code

By submitting a contribution, you acknowledge that the project may continue

If a contribution contains third-party material, the contributor must
identify it and provide its applicable license before submission. Third-party
material remains subject to its original license.
